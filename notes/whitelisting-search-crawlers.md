This repository features blocklists. However, while offering my personal blocklists for public use and consideration, I also encourage webmasters to avoid blocking independent search crawlers, quite a few of which are not used for any sort of AI training. Instead of going through my reasoning here, I point you to my 2023 *New Leaf Journal* essay: [On whitelisting independent search crawlers](https://thenewleafjournal.com/whitelisting-independent-search-crawlers/). In that article, I focus on Seznam, which is an independent Czech-based search engine (blocked by default in the current 8G Firewall, but not my modified version). I would also especially encourage webmasters to avoid blocking [Mojeek](https://www.mojeek.com/bot.html) and [Marginalia Search](https://about.marginalia-search.com/), both of which are important independent search projects. Note however that I originally suggested not blocking Common Crawl back in 2023, but I now have it on my blocklists because of its being used for AI training.
