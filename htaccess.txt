# Current .htaccess bot blocklist
# Inspired by https://github.com/ai-robots-txt/ai.robots.txt, from which I borrowed the structure
# This list is based on the ai-robots-txt repo, my own logs analysis, and https://knownagents.com/agents
# Categories derive from https://knownagents.com/agents
# My policy is to try to block AI training and wasteful/malicious bots and scrapers but allow search
